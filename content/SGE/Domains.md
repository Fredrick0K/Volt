
ERP -> Enterprise Resource Planning
- Modular
- Designed to enhance and fastening workflows
- Easy communication with Customers or Providers
Cons:
- Costs.
- Employees capacity to manage Software and work w it
Types:
- ERP on Cloud: ERP hosted in Cloud, PC clients connects to Cloud 
- Vertical: Created to solve issues in an exact sector, not compatible with others.
- Horizontal: Made to work for bigger variety of sectors
- Under License: (Paid)
- Pay On Demand.

## First Finance Apps in History

In the 60's, apps where developed for Finance management:
- BOM (**B**ill **O**f **M**aterials)
- IMC (**I**nventory **M**anagement **C**ontrol)
In the 70's :
- MRP (Material Requirements Planning)
- MRP II, developed in the 80's.
In the 90's:
- ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning)

In the future, new models of Business like:
	• e-commerce (B2C, B2B…): distribución, venta, compra, marketing y
	suministro de información de productos o servicios a través de
	Internet.
	• m-commerce: se trata de comercio electrónico usando el teléfono
	móvil, ya sea mediante un navegador o una aplicación como medio
	para la compra.
	• s-commerce: social commerce o comercio social es una de las
	mayores tendencias de comercio electrónico, y consiste en el uso de
	las redes sociales como canales de venta de productos
